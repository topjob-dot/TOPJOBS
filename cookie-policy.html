Cookie Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage used on topjobsseekers.com, and the third-party content the site loads.

1. What are cookies and similar storage?

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. Websites also use similar tools, such as your browser's local storage, to remember things between visits.

2. What we use

ItemWhat it doesHow long
Sign-in session (Supabase, stored in local storage)Keeps you signed in to the portal and dashboard.Until you log out or it expires
Chat session ID (tjs_chat_session_id)Lets the website assistant keep your conversation together.Until you clear your browser data
Notice choice (tjs_cookie_notice_v1)Remembers that you have seen the cookie notice.Until you clear your browser data

These are essential for the site to work as you expect. We do not currently use advertising cookies or analytics cookies. If we add them, we will update this page and ask for your consent first.

3. Content from other providers

To display the site, your browser loads fonts, icons, scripts and images from providers such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome (cdnjs), jsDelivr, Pexels and FlagCDN. These providers can see your IP address and browser details when this happens, and may set their own cookies. Please see their policies for details.

4. Links to other sites

Links to WhatsApp, employer application pages or social media take you to other services, which have their own cookie and privacy practices.

5. Managing your choices

  • You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Signing in and the chat history will reset.
  • You can re-open the cookie notice with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page.

6. Contact

Questions? Email topjobsseekers@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
