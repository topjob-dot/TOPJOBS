Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

TOPJOBSEEKERS ("we", "us", "our") is a recruitment agency based in Kenya that connects job seekers with employers in Kenya and abroad. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: full name, email address and phone number.
  • Profile details: country, education level and the job categories or destinations you are interested in.
  • Your CV and any information it contains, plus any message you write to us.
  • Employer details: company name and contact information, if you register as an employer.
  • Chat messages you send to our website assistant.
  • Technical data such as your browser type and IP address, which your browser sends whenever you visit a website.

2. How we use your information

  • To process your job applications and match you with suitable vacancies.
  • To contact you about your applications, interviews and next steps.
  • To share your CV with the employer when you apply for their role.
  • To answer your questions through email, WhatsApp or the website assistant.
  • To keep our website secure and to improve it.

We rely on your consent (for example when you submit a CV or tick the privacy box when you register) and on our need to provide the service you asked for.

3. Who can see your information

Your CV and application details are seen by our recruitment staff, and by the employer you apply to (or approved employer accounts on our platform). We do not sell your personal data. When you apply for a job abroad, your details are sent to an employer outside Kenya, and different data protection rules may apply there.

4. Service providers we use

We use trusted providers to run the website. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase for our database, user accounts and secure file storage (servers in Europe).
  • Resend to email your application to our recruitment team.
  • Google (Gemini) to generate replies in our website assistant. Messages you type into the assistant are sent to Google for this purpose, so please do not enter passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive details in the chat.
  • Content providers such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, jsDelivr, Pexels and FlagCDN, which deliver fonts, icons, scripts and images. They receive your IP address when your browser loads their content.
  • WhatsApp if you contact us or join our channel or group. WhatsApp has its own privacy policy.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your CV and details for as long as we need them to help you find work, or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

6. Security

Your CV is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored with access controls, so that job seekers can only see their own records. No online system is completely risk-free, so please use a strong password and keep it private.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw your consent, and receive a copy of it. To use any of these rights, email topjobsseekers@gmail.com. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

8. Children

Our services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Cookies

See our Cookie Policy for the storage we use on this website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

11. Contact
